Teams own projects. The creator is the team **owner**; everyone else joins
as a **member**.

## Creating a team

`POST /api/v1/teams` (requires a verified email). The owner can create
projects in the team and manage it.

## Invite links

The owner requests an invite link (`GET /api/v1/teams/:teamId/invite-link` —
permission-checked) and shares it. The link's token is validated publicly
(`GET /api/v1/teams/invite/:token`), then joining is a signed-in
`POST /api/v1/teams/:teamId/join`:

- owner joining their own team → `team_owner`;
- already a member → `already_a_team_member`;
- members join with **BASIC** permission.

## Leaving

`POST /api/v1/teams/:teamId/leave` — allowed for members. The owner cannot
leave (`team_owner`); ownership transfer is not available today.

## Deleting a team

`DELETE /api/v1/teams/:teamId` — owner only, and only for **non-personal**
teams (`is_personal = FALSE`). Deleting a team deletes its projects.

## Personal teams

Each account gets a personal team at signup (provisioned automatically);
personal teams cannot be deleted. Projects can live in any team you belong
to.
